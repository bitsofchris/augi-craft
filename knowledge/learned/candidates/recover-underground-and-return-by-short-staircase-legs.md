## Procedure

Use mineForDiamonds v23 or later. Carry cooked food and an iron pickaxe. At low health, allow its wall-in and eat recovery before branch mining. Follow recorded staircase waypoints in short climb legs on return; verify deposit counts rather than treating arrival as delivery.
